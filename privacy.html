Privacy and personal information.

How the website is intended to handle personal information, and what must be confirmed before forms go live.

WEBSITE POLICY

Draft for review — update this policy to match the final business and technology arrangements before enabling live forms or checkout.

1. What information may be collected

This website is currently a static information and preview site. The contact form on this preview is not connected to a submission service and should not be used to send personal or confidential information. When live forms are enabled, the information collected may include contact details, organisation details, enquiry category and the message you choose to provide.

2. Why information may be used

Information submitted through an operational form may be used to respond to enquiries, assess a service request, manage a business relationship, improve the website or meet legal obligations. The final purposes must be stated clearly before the form is activated.

3. Disclosure and service providers

Information may need to be shared with service providers or partners where necessary to respond to a request, and only under appropriate arrangements. The final policy should name or describe relevant form, hosting, analytics and payment providers actually used.

4. Storage, security and retention

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information against misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access. Retention periods, access controls, backups and deletion procedures must be documented for each operational system.

5. Access, correction and complaints

When a privacy contact pathway is established, individuals should be able to request access to or correction of their information and raise a privacy concern. Add the approved contact process before launch.

6. Cookies and analytics

This version does not intentionally include analytics tracking. If analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential cookies are introduced, update this policy and implement any required notices or consent controls.

7. Policy updates

This policy must be reviewed when forms, hosting, partners, analytics, store checkout or data-handling practices change.
