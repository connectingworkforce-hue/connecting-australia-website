WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy statement

Draft for review: This page describes intended privacy principles. Before publication, confirm that it accurately reflects the organisation's actual data collection, website technology, service providers and legal obligations.

1. Our approach to privacy

Connecting Australia aims to respect people's privacy and handle personal information responsibly. Information should be collected only where there is a legitimate purpose, handled securely and accessed only by people who need it.

2. Information you may provide

If you contact us by email or another communication method, you may provide your name, contact details, enquiry and any other information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, financial details or sensitive personal information unless a suitable, secure process has been confirmed.

3. Website technical information

Website hosting providers and third-party services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, access times and requested pages. This website currently references externally hosted fonts and an image. Those providers may receive technical requests from your browser when the resources load. The actual hosting logs, analytics and other technologies in use must be confirmed before this statement is finalised.

4. How information may be used

Information you submit may be used to respond to your enquiry, communicate with you about a requested service, maintain business records, protect the website and meet applicable legal obligations. Any additional use should be explained before information is collected.

5. Disclosure and service providers

Personal information should not be sold. Where information needs to be handled by a service provider or disclosed for a lawful purpose, the circumstances and relevant safeguards should be assessed and explained as required by law. The organisation's actual providers and arrangements need to be confirmed.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. Information should be retained only for as long as required for its purpose or applicable legal obligations. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Access, correction and privacy enquiries

For privacy enquiries, please use our contact form. Please avoid including sensitive information in your initial enquiry.

8. Australian privacy requirements

The privacy obligations that apply depend on the organisation, its activities and the circumstances of the information handling. This draft is not a legal determination of compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles or any other applicable law.

9. Updates to this statement

This statement may need to be updated when the website, services, technology or information-handling practices change. A publication date and a process for reviewing changes should be added before launch.

10. Contact

For privacy enquiries, please use our contact form. Please avoid including sensitive information in your initial enquiry.
