CONNECTING WORKFORCE

Prepare with confidence.

Practical checklists and links to support job search, interviews and workplace readiness.

01 / PLAN

Job-search plan

Set a weekly target for applications, employer research and follow-ups. Keep a simple record of roles and closing dates.

02 / APPLY

Résumé and application

Tailor your résumé to the role, use clear examples and check contact details and spelling before submitting.

03 / PREPARE

Interview preparation

Review the job ad, prepare examples of your skills, plan travel and write down questions for the employer.

04 / START

First-day checklist

Confirm start time, location, supervisor, clothing or PPE, transport, documents and any access requirements.

05 / LEARN

Training information

Check recognised training and course information with official providers and relevant authorities.

Workplace information
