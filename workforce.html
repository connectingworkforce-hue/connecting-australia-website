WORKFORCE SUPPORT

Practical guidance.
Your own next step.

We share general information and resources that may help people prepare for work and connect with employers. Support depends on the request and is not a government-funded employment program.

Build a practical plan

Start with your goals, strengths, availability and the type of work that may suit you. Break the process into manageable actions and keep a record of applications and follow-ups.

  • Identify roles that fit your skills and circumstances
  • Prepare a clear, relevant résumé
  • Practise interview and workplace conversations
  • Confirm transport, schedule and first-day details
